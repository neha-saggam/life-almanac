Ventures

Two ideas, both still on the side of a day job

The café

A side-investment café in Pune, bounded tightly: under ₹10L capital, a six-month risk window, ~10 hrs/week. Cloud kitchen or kiosk fits those limits best — though most cafés take 8–18 months to break even, longer than the risk window allows for.

idea → modeled → deciding → launched

The decor business

A style-categorized home decor line with her sister, an interior designer — decor grouped by look (Warm Minimal, Bohemian first) instead of product type, sourced through a vendor her sister already trusts, kept renter-friendly and movable.

idea → modeled → deciding → launched
Neither has launched. Both are being planned properly before a rupee goes in — that discipline is itself a pattern worth noticing.
